Novel Interaction: Design Reflection

I extended the Lab 9 geospatial visualization (choropleth + cartogram of 2025 GDP) with voice interaction using the browser's Web Speech API. Users can say commands such as "Highlight China", "Highlight Japan", or "Reset" to select and clear country highlights across both maps.

Voice is appropriate here because the map contains many small countries that are difficult to click precisely, especially after zooming. Voice lets users jump directly to a named country without hunting for its shape. The interaction is also naturally paired with the existing linked-highlighting feature — when a country is named, both the choropleth and the cartogram update simultaneously.

Feedback: The interface shows what was heard ("Heard: highlight china"), what action was taken ("Action: Highlighting china"), and offers suggestions if the command is not recognized. Conventional mouse controls remain fully functional as a fallback, and the microphone only activates after an explicit button click.

Limitations: Speech recognition can mishear accented speech or country names, and it is not supported in all browsers. Future improvements could include fuzzy matching and additional languages.
