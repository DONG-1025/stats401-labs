Analysis

Dataset: Sentiment140 (1.6 million tweets), sampled 2,000 tweets for analysis.
Records: 2,000 tweets after cleaning.
Cleaning decisions: Removed missing values and duplicates, standardized text formatting, converted numeric fields, and added derived temporal features (hour, weekday).
Sentiment method: RoBERTa-based Transformer model (cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest). Sentiment is model-generated and may not be ground truth.

Observations:
The sentiment distribution shows a relatively balanced mix, with Positive tweets (752) slightly outnumbering Negative (686) and Neutral (562). This suggests the sampled tweets lean mildly positive overall. The sentiment score ranges from approximately -1 to 1, where values closer to 1 indicate more positive sentiment.

The sentiment-by-weekday analysis reveals variation in average sentiment across days, with certain weekdays showing higher or lower sentiment scores. This could reflect differences in user mood or topics discussed on different days of the week.

While the RoBERTa model performs well on social media text, it may still misclassify sarcasm, irony, or ambiguous language. These sentiment estimates should be interpreted as approximate indicators rather than definitive ground truth.
